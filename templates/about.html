About StudentHub

Built for simple student management.

StudentHub keeps student records, marks and attendance organized and up to date, with separate access for admins, teachers and students.

Admin

Manages students, teachers, courses and subjects.

Teacher

Takes attendance and enters marks for their own subjects.

Student

Views their own marks and attendance.
